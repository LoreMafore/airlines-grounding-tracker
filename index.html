02 / THE FINE PRINT

Know what you’re
looking at.

01

Revenue, not losses

For sourced rows: FY2024 reported revenue for the named group, segment or product ÷ 365 ÷ 24 × 4h 26m. Different coverage bases and unsourced inputs mean these are not realized lost sales or profit.

02

The fixed window

FAA advisory 111 ↗ lists a start of Nov 13, 20:53 UTC. FAA cancellation 005 ↗ was signed Nov 14, 01:19 UTC. We use the signed cancellation time as the end, not the old advisory's planned expiry. Some departures were permitted before cancellation; recovery delays continued after it.

03

Why another site is higher

That model ↗ adds assumed tourism ($500m/day), passenger productivity ($300m/day), cargo ($150m/day) and regional airline revenue. Ours only sums listed airline rates and is now frozen at the FAA cancellation time.

Review or adjust model assumptions ↗

The official end time is fixed at Nov 14, 01:19 UTC. Adjusting the start or revenue rates creates a different estimate in your browser only; the published final estimate is unchanged. Breeze, Avelo and Atlas use explicitly illustrative rates. Hawaiian and Spirit remain excluded.
