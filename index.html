02 / THE FINE PRINT

Know what you’re
looking at.

01

Not a live financial feed

No airline has supplied these loss figures. Each number is a hypothetical hourly cost multiplied by elapsed time. The total is the sum of the listed carriers.

02

Time, not refreshes

Refreshing recalculates from the same UTC start time. It never adds a random amount per visit. The counter updates every second and stops at the configured end time.

03

Incident context ≠ proof

AIRLIVE’s Nov 13 report ↗ describes limited disruptions at other airlines, not a grounding of the U.S. carriers listed here. The start time and hourly rates are scenario assumptions.
