02 / THE FINE PRINT

Know what you’re
looking at.

01

Revenue, not losses

For sourced carriers: FY2024 reported group revenue ÷ 365 ÷ 24 × hours since the stop. This is an average revenue pace, not realized lost sales or profit. Unsourced inputs are labeled illustrative and included in the total.

02

Start & limitations

Start: Nov 13, 20:53 UTC (3:53 p.m. ET), the reported advisory window. CNBC confirms the FAA stop ↗. The model does not account for delays, recoveries, domestic share, or time of day; set an end time when the stop lifts.

03

Why another site is higher

That model ↗ adds assumed tourism ($500m/day), passenger productivity ($300m/day), cargo ($150m/day) and regional airline revenue. Ours only sums listed airline rates. Its start is 21:06 UTC; ours uses the earlier reported 20:53 UTC.

Adjust scenario assumptions ↗

Changes are saved in this browser only. Sourced rates start from FY2024 revenue divided by 8,760 hours; the other rates are explicitly illustrative. Set the actual end time when confirmed, or leave blank to keep projecting. Hawaiian and Spirit are fixed at $0 to prevent double counting and historical misattribution.
