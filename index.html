Not a live financial feed

No airline has supplied these loss figures. Each number is a hypothetical hourly cost multiplied by elapsed time. The total is the sum of the listed airlines.

02

Time, not refreshes

Refreshing recalculates from the same UTC start time. It never adds a random amount per visit. The counter updates every second and stops at the configured end time.

03

Limited confirmed impact

AIRLIVE’s Nov 13 report ↗ describes three SpiceJet cancellations and two delays, plus communications issues at other carriers. It does not establish a global fleet grounding or any financial loss total.
